## Overview

This thesis investigates how youth sports communities in Uppsala, Sweden make sense of a token-based digital wellbeing platform (Welloop), and whether those communities are ready to use tokens for circular exchange of used sports equipment. The circular exchange concept was proposed by the researchers and received positively by Welloop — it did not originate from the company.

**Research Questions**

- **RQ1:** How do young athletes and coaches make sense of the token system in their digital wellbeing platform?
- **RQ2:** Under what conditions are youth sports communities ready to use tokens for exchanging used sports equipment?

## Methodology

- **Framework:** Braun & Clarke (2006) six-phase inductive thematic analysis
- **Philosophy:** Interpretivism + social constructionism

- **Community readiness operationalised via:** Hassett et al. R=MC² framework
- **Coding workspace:** Excel (mechanical coding) + Python/Pandas (cross-tabulation)
- **Visualisation:** Power BI, Matplotlib

## Findings — Five Themes

| # | Theme | Primary RQ | Instances | Saturation |
|---|-------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| T1 | Conditional Readiness for Circular Exchange | RQ2 | 326 | 100% |
| T2 | Equity as the Legitimating Rationale | RQ2 + RQ1 | 157 | 100% |
| T3 | Trust as a Negotiated Condition | Both | 139 | 100% |
| T4 | Tokens as Dual-Purpose Instruments | RQ1 | 80 | 100% |
| T5 | Configuring Physical Handover within App-Mediated Coordination | RQ2 + RQ1 | 70 | 100% |

**Total coded instances:** 1,063 across 20 first-order codes

**Key analytical highlights:**
- `competition_pressure` — coach-only signal (10/10 instances from coaches, zero athletes)
- `trust_safety` — 100% participant and team saturation; strongest single code (131 instances)
- `fairness_reciprocity` — athlete-dominated (40/42 instances from athletes)
- Physical exchanges are always offline; app serves as coordination/communication layer only
- Equity reframed as a motivational foundation underpinning three readiness conditions, not a fourth condition

## Limitations

- No parent or club administrator interviews (8 clubs approached, non-responsive)
- All-male sample — findings not generalisable across gender
- IF U18 team is coach-only due to recruitment failure (treated as limitation, not delimitation)
- Single embedded case study — findings are contextually bounded to Uppsala, Sweden
